Walk-Up Music for Your Whole League

Every kid hears their name and their song when they step up to bat. Here is how a league can make that happen on every field, without buying equipment or managing a subscription.

What each team needs

  • One iPhone. Usually a coach or a team parent. There is no Android or iPad version today.
  • One Bluetooth speaker. Most teams already have one in someone's trunk.
  • The OnDeckDJ app. Free to download.

Setup takes about an hour per team before the season. At games it is one tap when a batter walks up and one tap when they are ready to hit.

What it costs

Nothing for the league. Each team decides for itself.

  • Free version: one team of up to 12 players, with 15 announcer credits. One credit makes one player's intro. Plenty of rec teams never pay.
  • Pro: a one-time $9.99 purchase on the phone that runs the music. Bigger rosters, more teams, and 200 credits. No subscription, no per-player fee.
  • Extra credits: $4.99 a pack, only if a team wants to keep remaking intros.

Parents never pay. With Team Link, they can follow the lineup from their own iPhone and suggest their child's song, free and without an account.

How leagues usually roll it out

  1. Decide your music rules first. Clean lyrics, a time limit per clip, no music while the ball is live, and a volume that stays on your own field. Write them down once and send them to every coach.
  2. Ask each team to name a music parent at the first team meeting. It is a popular volunteer job.
  3. Forward the setup guide. Our guide for team parents covers collecting songs, setup and game day in one page.
  4. Try it at opening day or one weekend of games before asking every team to join in.

Walk-up music rules are set locally. Check your own league rules, your field permits and any park rules about amplified sound.

Common questions

Can the league buy Pro for every team at once?

Not today. Apple sells Pro inside the app, one phone at a time. At $9.99 once, most teams cover it themselves or skip it.

Does it work for softball?

Yes. Baseball and softball, any age group, from tee ball through high school and adult leagues.

What if a coach changes mid-season?

The lineup can be exported and sent to the new coach's iPhone. The batting order and announcer intros come with it at no extra credit cost.

What about fields with weak signal?

Intros are made ahead of time at home and play without a connection. Songs imported as audio files play without a connection too, so they are the safer choice at fields with poor signal.
